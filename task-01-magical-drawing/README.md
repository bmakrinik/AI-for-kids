# Task 1: Magical Drawing World

**Age:** 4–8 (with a parent) · **Time:** 20–40 minutes · **Difficulty:** Beginner

## Goal

Turn a child's own drawings into a magical illustrated scene, then use follow-up requests to make creative changes. The child stays the creative director; AI is the tool.

## What you need

- Paper and crayons or markers
- A phone or camera
- An AI image tool that accepts uploaded photos and can generate/edit images (for example, ChatGPT with image creation)
- An adult to supervise uploads

## Step-by-step

1. **Draw two pictures.** Ask your child to draw a character or creature. A parent, sibling, or friend draws another one. Encourage bold colors and distinctive features.
2. **Photograph the drawings.** Place them on a flat surface in bright, even light. Keep the entire drawing visible and avoid shadows.
3. **Upload both photos to your image-capable AI tool.** Ask it to combine the two characters into one magical world, preserving what makes each drawing recognizable.
4. **Review together.** Ask your child: “What did the AI get right? What did it change?”
5. **Make one edit at a time.** Add a unicorn, moon, rainbow, castle, or another detail the child chooses. Ask the AI to preserve everything else.
6. **Compare versions.** Which one does your child like best, and why? Optionally print the final artwork.

## Copy-and-paste prompts

**First image:**

> These are two drawings made by a child and an adult. Combine the characters into one friendly magical world. Keep each character recognizable, including their original colors, shapes, and distinctive features. Create a colorful storybook scene suitable for a five-year-old. Do not add text.

**Follow-up edit:**

> Keep the characters and composition from the previous picture. Add a friendly golden unicorn standing beside them. Do not change anything else.

**Another edit:**

> Keep everything else the same. Add a small glowing moon in the sky.

**Personalization:**

> Add a handwritten-looking dedication that says “For [name], from [names]” in a clear place without covering the characters.

## What children learn

- **Input matters:** Clear pictures and specific instructions improve the result.
- **Iteration:** A first answer is a starting point, not the end.
- **Agency:** The child chooses what stays and what changes.
- **Critical thinking:** AI may reinterpret or invent details; the child can correct it.

## Questions to ask

- Which details came from your drawing, and which did the AI invent?
- How could we explain the change more clearly?
- Did the AI follow all our instructions?
- What would you change next?

## Troubleshooting

- **Character looks different:** Ask to preserve the exact number of eyes, colors, and shape.
- **Too many changes at once:** Request only one change per prompt.
- **Photo not recognized well:** Retake it with better lighting and a plain background.
- **Text misspelled:** Try a shorter dedication or add text in a separate editor.

## Safety

An adult should review uploaded photos and avoid including faces, names, addresses, or other identifying information unless appropriate and consented to. Keep the activity collaborative rather than leaving the child alone with an AI service.
